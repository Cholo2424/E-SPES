About the Program
Empowering Students Through Employment

SPES creates work opportunities for qualified students during summer and Christmas breaks — giving them a chance to earn income while completing their education and helping their families cope with financial hardship.

For Deserving Students

Priority given to financially disadvantaged students who need extra income while studying.

Co-Funded by Gov't

The government covers part of your salary — employers only need to pay a portion of the wage.

During School Breaks

Work opportunities are offered during summer and Christmas vacation so it won't affect your studies.

Application Process
How to Apply

Complete these four steps to register as a SPES beneficiary in Calamba City.

1

Check Eligibility

Make sure you meet the age, income, and enrollment requirements before proceeding.

2

Prepare Documents

Gather your school ID, income papers, district certificate, and other requirements.

3

Submit Application

Go to the IIPES Office or apply through this portal during the open application period.

4

Get Deployed

Once approved, you'll be assigned to a partner office and start working.

Who Can Apply
Eligibility Requirements

The program prioritizes students who need financial assistance the most.

  • Currently enrolled in any secondary or tertiary institution
  • Family annual income does not exceed ₱250,000
  • Between 15 to 25 years of age
  • Filipino citizen and Calamba City resident
  • Passing grades in current enrollment period
  • Has not previously benefited (priority to first-timers)

College Students

Enrolled in any recognized college or university

Senior High Students

Grade 11–12 in public or private schools

Out-of-School Youth

Youth who dropped out but intend to return

Dependents of DWs

Children of displaced workers from prior year
